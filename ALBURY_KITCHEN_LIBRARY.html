Authority And Opening Baseline

This file owns the use and ownership of Albury's marked household goods, overnight welcome provision, stationery packs and selected guest gifts at the 11 August 2025 opening. It supersedes fixed departure tiers and older image captions that imply every dinner guest receives an identical bag. ALBURY_CULINARY_PROGRAMME.md owns recipes, food handling and commercial status; ALBURY_MENUS_AND_COMMON_FOODS.md owns menus; PROPERTY_ALBURY_STAFFING.md owns staff and reporting.

Albury is Alex's home, built to welcome friends and entertain. The house mark identifies household equipment as well as personal gifts; it does not mean everything in a room is a souvenir. Provision is generous, discreet and adapted to the guest. These are opening capabilities and ordinary service arrangements, not new booked visits, an account of gifts already accepted, or a retail launch. The existing five weekend visitors remain the established opening visitor group.

Proper Guest Stationery Pack

The take-home pack is a compact bookcloth presentation folio containing:

  • One A5 clothbound notebook and one pocket notebook.
  • One metal pen and one graphite pencil.
  • Ten sheets of ivory writing paper and ten matching envelopes.
  • Four correspondence cards and two Albury street-elevation postcards.
  • One slim leather bookmark.

The folio, notebook covers and custom paper goods carry the architectural house mark. The whole pack is the guest's. It is distinct from the permanent leather correspondence folder in the bedroom. A small 'Yours to keep' card can make ownership clear. Wi-Fi, contact extensions and service information are supplied separately on the room-information card; no invented password, phone number or service timetable is fixed here.

Overnight Arrival Hamper

Before an overnight arrival, a reusable lidded wicker basket is placed on the guest-room console. It has a washable Albury-marked linen lining and a restrained leather handle/strap treatment. It is a room welcome basket, distinct from the picnic hamper and from a gifted food basket.

The standard summer opening arrangement provides:

  • One 500 ml Limestone Springs Still Water and one 500 ml Limestone Springs Sparkling Water, in their own blue-labelled glass bottles with silver screw caps.
  • One small labelled jar of Bethan's butter shortbread, P21.
  • One small labelled jar of dark-chocolate and sea-salt sablés, P22.
  • One small savoury snack jar, normally rosemary and smoked-salt almonds, P23; adjusted for dietary needs or preferences.
  • A small seasonal fruit selection, illustrated with a peach and grapes.
  • A personal welcome note and the complete guest stationery pack, tucked alongside the basket when that keeps food and paper comfortably separated.

The food, waters and stationery are for the guest; the basket remains with the room. A couple shares one sensibly sized basket with sufficient food and drink; personal stationery and travel items follow the actual guests. Replenishment follows use, not a daily ceremonial replacement of the entire hamper. Returning friends receive familiar favourites and fresh consumables without automatic duplicate keepsakes.

Robes, slippers, bathroom supplies and bedside water are already in their normal places. A chosen travel cup or reusable bottle is an additional personal gift, not compulsory in every arrival basket. House cordial and any other preparation requiring chilling wait in the guest refrigerator or are served chilled; the welcome note can mention them. Cream Soda or another known favourite may be provided alongside the standard waters. No wine or alcohol is assumed for every guest.

Take-Home Food Hamper

A separate food gift is assembled for the guest and journey, using a marked gift basket or suitable carrier. This basket and the packed contents belong to the recipient; it is not the room basket removed from stock.

The illustrated six-item example contains Seville marmalade (P01), bourbon and black cherry confiture (P05, with Hatfield Original credited), house mustard (P09), seeded rye crackers (P19), butter shortbread (P21), and rosemary/smoked-salt almonds (P23). It is an example of the available repertoire, not a mandatory six-product formula. Ketchup, other preserves, biscuits, snacks or cordial can be chosen. Chilled goods need a suitable journey and cold provision; nothing becomes shelf-stable because it is wrapped as a gift. Individual gifts use appropriate contents, batch/date, storage and allergen information.

Dinner guests may leave with a small food gift, a useful house object or nothing formal. The old 'Tier Two' photograph remains an optional assortment of available items; the mandatory fixed ten-item departure rule is retired. Staying guests can have their personal provisions and remaining treats packed for them. No guest must perform gratitude or attend a gift presentation for the scene to move on.
