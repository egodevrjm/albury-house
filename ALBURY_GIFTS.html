Candle & biscuits.

The scent of the house, and something from Bethan’s kitchen. A small comfort for the end of a long day.

The breakfast parcel

A slower morning.

Two preserves, house granola and a favourite mug. Something to open on a birthday, or the morning after a very good weekend.

  • Seville marmalade
  • Strawberry & vanilla confiture
  • Toasted oat, almond & seed granola

The pictured preserves can be changed for the recipient’s favourites. The mug stays long after breakfast.

The cordial pair

Pour something good.

A fruity favourite and a sharper companion, for a long drink with soda or something from the bar.

  • Raspberry & lemon verbena cordial
  • Pink grapefruit & rosemary cordial
  • Two etched house highball tumblers
  • Two fresh Albury coasters
  • A card with alcohol-free and cocktail serving ideas

Sealed gift-batch cordials, ready to send by courier or post. Store unopened in a cool, dry place; refrigerate after opening. Batch and storage details are included.

A small act of hospitality

Packed here.
Sent with a note.

Alex chooses the person, the occasion and what he wants to say. Crispin and the household team arrange the selection, packing and delivery, with Rafael and Bethan preparing the food.

The tin fits neatly into a protective postal box; the wicker hampers travel in fitted outer cartons. Courier or post is chosen for the contents and journey. The cordial pair uses protective fitted compartments for its bottles and tumblers.

For a particular favourite or dietary preference, ask the team. They will choose what is suitable and ready to send.
