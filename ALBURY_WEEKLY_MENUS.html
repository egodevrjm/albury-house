A week at the Albury table. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, with Bethan’s bread, baking and desserts.

MondayTuesdayWednesdayThursdayFridaySaturdaySunday

Every morning

The day’s hot breakfast, fruit and baking are served to appetite. Coffee and tea are available; Alex’s usual double espresso follows breakfast.

At lunch & dinner

Limestone Springs still and sparkling water, properly brewed Cream Soda and the house’s chilled ginger–lemongrass or passion-fruit–lime cordial. Coffee or tea afterwards; wine when wanted.

A kitchen plan for a living house

This is the complete seven-day August menu cycle. The kitchen scales service to whoever is home; a lunch out or a night away does not mean a meal has to be served. Guest dietary needs are agreed before service, with the vegetarian plates already planned.

Bread, pastry and pantry production follow the kitchen rota, supported by specialist suppliers. Fruit, cheese, simple late suppers and staff meals remain part of normal household provision.
