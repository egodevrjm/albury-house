Every morning

The day’s hot breakfast, fruit and baking are served to appetite. Coffee and tea are available; Alex’s usual double espresso follows breakfast.

At lunch & dinner

Limestone Springs still and sparkling water, a choice of stocked soft drinks and the house’s chilled ginger–lemongrass or passion-fruit–lime cordial. Coffee or tea afterwards; wine when wanted.

A kitchen plan for a living house

The kitchen can scale lunch and dinner for friends and family. The vegan options include dishes kept on hand and others the team can prepare with notice.

Bread, pastry and pantry production follow the kitchen rota, supported by specialist suppliers. Fruit, cheese, simple late suppers and staff meals remain part of normal household provision.
